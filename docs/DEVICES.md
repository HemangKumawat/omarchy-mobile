# Supported devices

The status of each device, as verified on real hardware. Rows in the matrix have merged notes behind them; ports still in review are listed under [Wanted](#wanted) with their issue or pull request. What's still missing and the order we plan to tackle it: [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md). To add a device or update a cell, follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) §6.4: only claim what you've run on your own phone, and link the notes entry that shows it.

**Legend:**

- **works**: verified on the phone, on by default;
- **testing**: works by hand, not yet on by default;
- **partial**: works on by default, with a known gap that the notes name;
- **WIP**: bring-up in progress, some gates passed;
- **built**: driver and DT written and built, not yet run on the phone;
- **boots**: the block's firmware runs, but nothing user-facing uses it yet;
- **no**: not started, or blocked;
- **-**: the hardware doesn't exist on this device.
